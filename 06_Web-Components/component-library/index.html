EXPLORE

Accordion

Organise detailed information into sections learners can open when they need it.

What makes an effective learning interaction?

An effective interaction has a clear purpose, simple instructions, meaningful learner action, and feedback that supports the learning objective.

When should I use an accordion?

Use it for related information that learners may want to explore selectively. Avoid hiding essential instructions or making learners open many panels to complete a task.

What should I avoid?

Avoid vague labels, excessive nesting, and content that is difficult to compare because it is spread across closed panels.

COMPARE

Tabbed content

Present a small set of related topics without overwhelming the page.

Start with purpose

Every interaction should help the learner practise, apply, retrieve, or reflect on something that matters.

Make the learner act

Ask learners to make a choice, reveal an answer, sort information, or explain a decision—not simply click to continue.

Close the learning loop

Feedback should explain the result and guide the learner towards the intended understanding or next step.

Scenario-based learning

Work through a realistic invoice rejection. Choose an action, review the consequences, and decide what to do next.

YOUR ROLE · EBILLING ASSOCIATE

A client has rejected an invoice because the timekeeper rate does not match its records. The invoice is due for resubmission today. The matter profile shows $400 per hour, while the client’s rejection note refers to an approved rate of $425 per hour.

Choose the safest next action. You can retry if your first choice is not appropriate.

DECISION 1 OF 2

What should you do first?

DECISION 2 OF 2

You locate a documented approval for $425 per hour, effective from the start of the current billing period. The matter profile still shows $400. What is the best next step?

SCENARIO COMPLETE

Resolve the cause before resubmitting.

You verified the approval, corrected the matter setup through the approved process, and validated the invoice before resubmission.

Key learning

Do not treat a rejected invoice as an isolated data-entry problem. Verify the source approval, effective date, matter setup, and invoice details. Keep a clear record of the correction and follow the required review process.
